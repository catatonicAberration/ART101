Hello.

It is nice to see you again.

I wanted to show you somewhere new...

But I am having trouble deciding where.

There is just too many options.

Here, let me show you what I mean.

Look to your right. Notice how the web of nerves converges on a few select points. How they form large bulbs where they meet. Those bulbs hold small snapshots of memories of the old world.

Imagine for a moment, you have a small section of any given part of the world. It doesn't matter where, just that it is small and limited. Now, imagine that this small piece made up your entire concept of the world. It is all you know. All you would ever know.

Imagine building an entire world with that piece as your guide.

Everywhere in this vast web. In the center of every bulb. thousands of new worlds are born like this, every single day. So many choices to make, and near infinite possibilities before us. It's hard to decide where to go first.

It took me quite a while to get to this cluster.

Let's hope my indecision doesn't delay us further.
